Our journey

Two decades of decisions that mattered

From a two-partner disputes practice to a full-service consultancy — the milestones that shaped how we work today.

2003

Two partners, one office

Stackly opens as a small disputes practice in downtown New York, taking on the cases larger firms wouldn't return calls about.

2009

Corporate & real estate desks open

Client demand pulls us into formation, financing and property law — the first step toward a full-service model.

2014

Family & IP practice groups

We add family law and intellectual property, and adopt the fixed-fee model that still defines every engagement.

2019

ISO 27001 certification

An encrypted client portal and certified document handling make confidentiality a system, not a promise.

2026

480+ matters, one standard

Six practice groups, one point of contact — the same partner-led attention we started with in 2003.
